SUPPORT

Help & Support

How do I place an order?

Browse products, open a product and choose Buy. Enter your quantity, delivery address and map location. Review the estimate before placing your order.

How are delivery charges estimated?

Delivery is estimated at ₹{{ delivery_rate }} per kilometre of straight-line distance from pickup to delivery. Actual road distance may differ.

Why is my order waiting for logistics?

A suitable driver must accept your delivery request. Open My Orders and refresh the order details to check for an assignment.

Where can I see my driver?

Open your order after a driver is assigned. Driver contact details and available location updates appear there. An older GPS location is labelled as the last known location.
