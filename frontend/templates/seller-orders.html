How your orders work

1. Buyer places an order

The buyer purchases one of your listed products and the required quantity is reserved from your inventory.

2. Logistics is assigned

AgriConnect finds an eligible logistics provider based on load capacity and route compatibility.

3. Order is delivered

Follow the order through pickup, transportation and final delivery to the buyer.
