PM-KISAN

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Samman Nidhi

Financial Support

PM-KISAN provides direct income support to eligible landholding farmer families. The assistance helps farmers meet agricultural expenses such as seeds, fertilizers and other farming requirements.

Benefit: ₹6,000 per year in three installments

Eligibility: Eligible landholding farmer families

View Details Visit Official Website ↗

PMFBY

Pradhan Mantri Fasal Bima Yojana

Crop Insurance

PMFBY offers crop insurance protection against eligible crop losses caused by natural calamities, pests and diseases. It helps farmers reduce the financial impact of crop failure.

Benefit: Financial protection against crop loss

Eligibility: Eligible farmers as per scheme guidelines

View Details Visit Official Website ↗
KCC

Kisan Credit Card

Agricultural Credit Support

Credit Support

The Kisan Credit Card scheme provides farmers with easier access to agricultural credit. It supports cultivation expenses and other eligible agricultural and allied activities.

Benefit: Accessible and affordable agricultural credit

Eligibility: Eligible farmers involved in agriculture

View Details Visit Official Website ↗

Soil Health Card

Soil Health Management

Soil Management

The Soil Health Card provides information about the nutrient status of farm soil. It helps farmers select suitable fertilizers and improve soil health for better and sustainable crop production.

Benefit: Soil testing and fertilizer recommendations

Eligibility: Farmers seeking soil health information

View Details Visit Official Website ↗

PM-KUSUM

Pradhan Mantri Kisan Urja Suraksha

Renewable Energy

PM-KUSUM promotes the use of solar energy in agriculture. It supports the installation of solar pumps and renewable-energy solutions for farming and irrigation activities.

Benefit: Support for eligible solar agriculture projects

Eligibility: Eligible farmers, groups and agricultural users

View Details Visit Official Website ↗

AIF

Agriculture Infrastructure Fund

Infrastructure Support

The Agriculture Infrastructure Fund supports the development of agricultural infrastructure such as warehouses, cold storage units, grading facilities, pack houses and post-harvest management projects. It helps farmers reduce crop losses, improve storage and receive better prices for their produce.

Benefit: Financial support and interest subsidy for eligible projects

Eligibility: Farmers, FPOs, cooperatives and eligible agri-entrepreneurs
